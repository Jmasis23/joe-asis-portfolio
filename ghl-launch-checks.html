What the 10 checks cover

The parts of a launch that usually get skipped because the builder says everything looks fine.

  1. Lead intake from the real form, not the builder preview
  2. Duplicate behavior when the same lead comes back
  3. Speed-to-lead timing on the first message
  4. Stop conditions when a lead replies or opts out
  5. Unhappy paths: missed calls, no-shows, partial bookings
  6. Pipeline handoffs between stages and owners
  7. Calendar reality: real bookings, real reminders
  8. Mobile messages on an actual phone
  9. Attribution: source and campaign tracking that survives
  10. Proof-of-launch evidence you can show the client
